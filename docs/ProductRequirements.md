# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Name

Incident Vault

---

## Vision

Incident Vault is a secure platform that enables individuals to privately document incidents, preserve digital evidence, organize events into chronological timelines, and generate structured reports.

The platform prioritizes privacy, security, accessibility, and user control while laying the foundation for future AI-powered insights.

---

## Target Users

- Students
- Employees
- Women
- Men
- LGBTQ+ individuals
- Senior citizens
- Anyone who wishes to securely document incidents

---

## Goals

- Secure incident documentation
- Preserve evidence
- Generate organized reports
- Maintain user privacy
- Build a scalable platform for future AI integration

---

## Non-Goals (Version 1)

The following features are **not** included in Version 1:

- AI Incident Classification
- OCR
- Speech-to-text
- Blockchain verification
- Anonymous reporting
- Trusted contacts
