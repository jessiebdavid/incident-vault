# Incident Vault

> A secure platform for documenting incidents, preserving digital evidence, and supporting safe reporting with future AI-powered analysis.



## Overview

Incident Vault is designed to help individuals securely document incidents such as harassment, bullying, discrimination, abuse, stalking, cybercrime, or workplace misconduct.

Users can privately record incidents, upload supporting evidence, organize events into a chronological timeline, and generate structured reports when needed.

The long term vision is to integrate AI-powered analysis while keeping user privacy, security, and consent at the core of the platform.



## Problem Statement

Many people do not report harmful incidents immediately due to fear, trauma, lack of support, or insufficient evidence.

As time passes:
- Important details are forgotten.
- Evidence becomes scattered or lost.
- Building a clear timeline becomes difficult.

Incident Vault provides a secure digital space where users can safely preserve incident records until they choose how to use them.
